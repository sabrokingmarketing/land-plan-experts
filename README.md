# Land Plan Experts

Our Story
The firm was established in 2018 and the principle Bertus van Tonder has more than 30 year experience in land use planning. Our team of planners specialize in any type of land use application and we strive to provide our clients with excellent service and expertise. The firm is a member of The South African Association of Professional Planners (SAACPP). All planners at the firm are registered with the South African Council for Planners (SACPLAN).
